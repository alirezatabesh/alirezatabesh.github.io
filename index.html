About Me

I am Alireza, a Software Architect and Software Engineer focused on designing and building scalable, maintainable, and production-ready software systems.

Throughout my professional journey, I have worked on enterprise applications, backend architectures, distributed systems, and complex software solutions. My experience includes designing service-oriented architectures, Microservices, working with the .NET ecosystem, databases, messaging systems, and building solutions that can perform reliably in real-world production environments.

Beyond software development, I have a strong interest and hands-on experience in DevOps and Cloud Native technologies. I have worked with technologies such as Docker, Kubernetes, container orchestration, CI/CD pipelines, deployment strategies, runtime environments, and tools that support the complete software delivery lifecycle. I believe a software architecture is not complete unless it considers the entire journey from design and development to deployment, operation, and maintenance.

In recent years, I have been focusing on applied Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, and exploring how AI can improve the way we design, develop, and maintain software systems. I am interested in applying these technologies in practical and engineering-driven ways rather than just following trends.

My passion in software engineering is finding the right balance between simplicity, quality, scalability, maintainability, and real business needs. For me, great software is not just code that works; it is a system that can evolve over time, be understood by engineers, and deliver long-term value.

On this website, I share my experiences, technical articles, experiments, and lessons learned in software architecture, backend development, DevOps, Cloud Native technologies, and emerging technologies in software engineering.

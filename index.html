About Me

I am Alireza, a Software Architect and Software Engineer focused on designing and building scalable, maintainable, and production-ready software systems.

Throughout my professional journey, I have worked on enterprise applications, backend architectures, distributed systems, and complex software solutions. My experience includes service-oriented architectures, Microservices, the .NET ecosystem, databases, messaging systems, and building reliable systems for real-world production environments.

Beyond software development, I have strong hands-on experience in DevOps and Cloud Native technologies including Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, deployment strategies, and modern software delivery practices.

I believe software architecture is not complete unless it considers the entire lifecycle of a system — from design and development to deployment, operation, monitoring, and maintenance.

Recently, I have been focusing on Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and practical ways to integrate AI into software engineering workflows.

For me, great software is not just code that works. It is a system that can evolve over time, remain understandable by engineers, and deliver long-term business value.
